# Cali_FID research notes

Collected August 22, 2026 from public pages and archival indexes. Claims below are attributed because third-party listings can become stale.

## Identity and purpose

- **Name:** Cali_FID Parrot & Exotics Rescue Sanctuary.
- **Meaning of the name:** the former About page says “Cali_FID” is shorthand for “California Feathered Kids”; “fid” is a nickname some parrot owners use for their birds.
- **Location:** Modesto, California. Public directory and fundraising pages list 2118 Davis Way, Modesto, CA 95351.
- **Status:** the organization describes itself as a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit licensed by California. Mightycause lists EIN **45-2433107**. A historical event listing says nonprofit status was received in May 2011.
- **Focus:** rescue, foster care, rehabilitation, adoption, lifelong sanctuary, educational outreach, and conservation for exotic animals sold in the pet trade.
- **Species mentioned:** parrots and other pet birds, turtles, tortoises, snakes, lizards, amphibians, and other exotics.

## History and people

The former About page says Jennifer Perez began the rescue informally in 1998 after seeing a need for specialized parrot and exotic-animal rescue while volunteering with dog and cat rescues and a county shelter. The same page identifies veterinarian Brandon Barrett, DVM, as a collaborator who helped with non-domestic animals collected by local animal-control officers. It describes Cali_FID as the first 501(c)(3) exotic-species rescue in the San Joaquin Valley.

## Operating model and mission

The older homepage describes a foster-home-based program staffed by knowledgeable volunteers. Its stated mission was to improve standards of care through education, rehabilitation, adoption, and sanctuary. Its goal was to improve each animal’s mental and physical health, assess individual needs, and place the animal in foster care, an adoptive home, or permanent sanctuary as appropriate.

The live homepage currently says the site is being rebuilt and gives **califidrescue@gmail.com** for questions. An older fundraising directory lists **califid@gmail.com** and **209-988-3506**; those older details should be verified before use.

## Adoption and intake

The former adoption page required potential adopters to demonstrate suitable housing and care knowledge, provide an appropriate cage or enclosure, supply veterinary and personal references, sign a contract, and pay a nonrefundable adoption fee. It reserved the right to decline any adoption.

Petfinder’s Cali_FID listings add that animals are not shipped, adoptions must be completed in person, fees vary by species and needs, and applicants should submit the website application plus enclosure photos before arranging a meeting. The indexed homepage later stated that foster homes were full and the rescue was not accepting new animals; intake availability should therefore be confirmed directly.

## Programs, outreach, and support

- The former site described educational visits to schools, children’s clubs, fairs, special events, and some birthday parties.
- Named partners included Petco, local veterinarians, Exotic Animal Club for Kids, CV Herp Society, Sacramento Turtle & Tortoise Club, wildlife rehabilitators, educators, and other rescues.
- The older homepage reported a **$15,000 Petco Love grant** and a **$600 Friends of Front Street Shelter grant**.
- A sponsorship page offered a program beginning at $10/month to support special-needs parrots and reptiles who could not be adopted or released.
- The former donation page mentioned PayPal, AmazonSmile (a discontinued Amazon program), and an Amazon wish list. Current donation methods must be rechecked.

## Public listings and examples

- Mightycause repeats the mission and publishes nonprofit data including EIN 45-2433107.
- Petfinder identifies Cali_FID as a Modesto rescue and, at the time researched, displayed a Budgerigar named Candy with a $25 adoption fee. Individual animal availability is inherently temporary.
- More Reptiles lists Cali_FID as accepting lizards, snakes, tortoises, turtles, and amphibians.
- A 2018 event-directory entry described Saturday adoptions at the Modesto Petco at 2021 Evergreen Avenue and intake from shelters, veterinary clinics, pet stores, and private citizens. Treat schedule and venue as historical.
- Bettaworld for Bettas includes Cali_FID in its California fish-rescue map and identifies an Instagram presence as `@califid`.

## Reliability notes

The organization’s own pages are the primary sources for its mission and history, while external directories corroborate identity, services, and nonprofit details. Directory addresses, phone numbers, adoption schedules, animal listings, and intake status may be obsolete. Archive screenshots are documentary reproductions, not endorsements, and some may show Wayback error pages or missing assets when replay dependencies were not preserved.
